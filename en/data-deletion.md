# TrackCapture Data Deletion

TrackCapture has no sign-up and no servers, so Matelog keeps no user data. All data lives only on your device.

## Delete one activity
In the app: **History** tab → open the activity → Delete

## Delete all app data
- iPhone: touch and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App
- Android: Settings → Apps → TrackCapture → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall)

## Results saved to your photo library
Photos and videos the app saved to your photo library stay there after the app is deleted. Delete them in your Photos/Gallery app.

## Shared content
Delete content you shared to other services (such as Instagram) in those services.
