# TrackCapture Privacy Policy

Matelog ("we") operates the mobile app **TrackCapture** ("the app") in accordance with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

**In short:** the app has no sign-up and no servers of ours. Your activities, locations, photos and videos are **stored only on your device** and are never sent to us.

## 1. Information handled and purpose

| Information | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Location (GPS coordinates, altitude, time) | Recording your route, calculating distance, pace and speed, drawing the route on photos and videos | Your device |
| Photos and videos | In-app camera capture, adding your stats to photos and videos you pick | Your device (photo library) |
| Workouts from health apps (Apple Health, Health Connect) | Importing the route, distance and time of workouts you choose | Your device |
| GPX files | Importing route files you choose | Your device |
| App settings (language, units, templates) | Running the app | Your device |

We do not collect names, contact details, accounts, advertising IDs or any information that identifies you, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

## 2. Permissions

| Permission | Required? | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Location (while using the app) | Needed for recording | Recording your route during an activity. We never ask for "Always" access |
| Camera | Needed for capture | Taking photos and videos in the app |
| Photos and videos | Optional | Saving results, picking photos and videos |
| Notifications | Optional | Showing recording status (Android ongoing notification) |
| Health data (read) | Optional | Importing watch workouts. The app never writes to health apps |

You can use every other feature without the optional permissions, and you can change permissions in your device settings at any time.

## 3. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete an activity in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored. Results you saved to your photo library must be deleted from the photo library. We keep no data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

## 4. Third parties and external services

We do not share your information with third parties. The app does communicate with external services in these cases:

- **Maps:** to draw maps the app requests map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). The map area you are viewing and your device's IP address may be sent to that service. Your activity data is not sent.
- **Sharing:** when you tap Instagram Stories or Share, the photo or video you chose is passed to the app you select (such as Instagram). That service's policy then applies.

We do not outsource any processing of personal information.

## 5. Location information

The app uses location only while you are recording and never sends it to us or anyone else. Because no location data leaves your device, we keep no records of its use or provision. See the [Terms of Use](./terms) for location-based service terms.

## 6. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your activities in the app, and delete everything by deleting the app. See [Data deletion](./data-deletion). Contact us at the address below and we will reply without delay.

## 7. Children

The app collects no personal information and is not directed at children under 14.

## 8. Security

Data is stored in each operating system's app-private storage, which other apps cannot access. Nothing is sent to our servers, so there is no exposure in transit.
